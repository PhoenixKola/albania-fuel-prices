# November correctness release and growth follow-up

Source publication (`as_of`) is not the download time (`fetched_at_utc`). Per-market `source_as_of` takes precedence; Kosovo collection-only fallback has `last_observed_at` and cannot trigger alerts. Legacy history contains collection snapshots and later accepted publications: provenance cannot be reconstructed reliably, so no raw observations were deleted, re-dated or invented. Build-time current analysis is bounded at each accepted market reading. Earlier rows are collection observations, not verified daily publications. New Kosovo publications are stored in `market_observations` independently, and are included in trends without assigning Europe's date.

Calendar comparisons select the closest observation at or before the target, allowing at most seven days of additional gap; insufficient coverage is unavailable. Weekly changes use 7-14 days; 30-day trends retain a 37-day lookback buffer. Charts label their actual period and final chart reading rather than claiming a mismatched current price. Source-age monitoring warns after 10 days, configurable with SOURCE_AGE_WARNING_DAYS, separately from deployment integrity. The existing conflict issue remains deduplicated.

SEO files: web/scripts/priceData.ts (honest period/change and observed-date labels), web/scripts/prerender.ts (observed-date metadata), web/src/pages/CountryFuelPricesPage.tsx (same visible date and comparison guards), plus generated analysis content. Existing successful titles, canonicals, routing and language indexing are preserved. No indexed language shells were created. Review page-filtered Search Console queries before title experiments or a fully translated reciprocal sq URL pilot. Baseline supplied by owner, Oct 16-Nov 12: 164 clicks / 10,841 impressions / 1.51% CTR / position 7.6; preceding comparison 155 clicks / 17,697 impressions. Compare equivalent future periods by page, country, device and query; no immediate ranking gain is promised.
