## Search and product evidence

The owner's visible 1,000-row query sample includes `dieselpreis albanien` (109 impressions), `diesel price in albania` (73), `prezzo gasolio albania oggi` (38), `dieselprijs albanie vandaag` (20), `cmimi i naftes sot` (28), `gas stations open near me` (910) and `fuel near my location` (390). These incomplete rows cannot be assigned to a URL or summed into complete demand. Searcher country is not query language. Page observations: `/compare` 596 impressions/2 clicks, `/trip-cost-calculator` 373/0, `/stations` 3,693/1, while the homepage and Kosovo page already earn 63 and 54 clicks respectively.

On 20 October, broad web-result samples for English, Albanian, German, Italian and Dutch phrases were inspected without a fixed search-country or device setting; **SERP rank and map-pack composition remain unverified**. [HowAlbania's fuel-station guide](https://howalbania.com/fuel-stations-in-albania/) and [Cria's Albania trip calculator](https://cria.al/trip-planner/trip-cost-calculator) answer ordinary route-cost questions. [Derivate](https://www.derivatet.com/) advertises station-level Albania/Kosovo prices, which our dataset does not provide or verify. [Fillzz's detour guide](https://fillzz.com/guides/is-a-fuel-detour-worth-it) demonstrates that the *additional-distance cost* is a distinct calculation from a per-litre comparison. These are competitor/product observations, not proof of their data quality or measured search volume. The [Cargopedia Europe table](https://www.cargopedia.net/europe-fuel-prices) is our dated reference source. Google recommends original helpful content and accurate structured data; neither guidance promises rankings ([helpful content](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content), [structured-data policy](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/sd-policies)).

## Candidate score and selection

Each dimension is 0–5. Weighted score = demand 25%, data fit 25%, distinct value 20%, observed feasibility 15%, maintainability 10%, low risk 5%. Scores are judgment, not forecasts.

| Candidate | Demand | Data fit | Distinct | Feasible | Maintainable | Low risk | Total / 5 | Decision |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| User-entered extra-distance fuel-only calculator | 3 | 4 | 5 | 3 | 4 | 4 | **3.80** | **BUILD NEW**, one URL |
| Add a cash-to-driving-range widget to existing trip tool | 1 | 5 | 3 | 3 | 4 | 4 | 3.15 | RESEARCH FURTHER; weak measured demand |
| Add another generic 50 L border article/page | 3 | 4 | 1 | 3 | 4 | 1 | 2.85 | REJECT; duplicates Compare and archived article |
| Two DE/IT/NL or SQ localized country shells | 3 | 4 | 2 | 2 | 1 | 1 | 2.60 | RESEARCH FURTHER; full locale system required |
| Programmatic station/city price pages | 3 | 0 | 1 | 1 | 0 | 0 | 1.10 | REJECT; cannot satisfy retail-price intent |
| New road-status route pages | 1 | 0 | 1 | 1 | 0 | 0 | 0.60 | REJECT; manual review is stale |

The chosen page answers one question the existing pages do not: **how much of an illustrative national reference-price gap remains after *user-entered* extra driving fuel?** It will show gross gap, extra-driving litres/cost and net fuel-only difference. The user supplies *all* additional distance and consumption; no route, border station, toll, time or road condition is inferred. It will never call a positive number a guaranteed saving or recommend crossing a border. Stale or unverified market dates remain prominent. Existing `/compare` and the archived `/insights/cross-border-fill-up-math` retain their current ownership and metadata; only a contextual link will point to the new task.
