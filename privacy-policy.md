# Privacy Policy — Karburanti Sot

_Last updated: 2026-09-06_

Karburanti Sot (“the App”) displays public fuel price information, allows you to save basic preferences, and provides an optional feature for finding fuel stations near your location.

## What data we collect

The App does not require an account and does not ask for personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or passwords.

### Location

The App can use your device's precise location when you choose to use the **nearby stations** feature.

Location access is optional. The App requests location permission only when needed for this feature.

If you grant permission, your device coordinates are sent securely to our station lookup service at:

`fuel-proxy.fenixkola.workers.dev`

The coordinates may also be processed by third-party mapping and geographic data services, such as OpenStreetMap / Overpass, in order to find fuel stations near your location and calculate their distance from you.

Your location is used only to provide nearby station results. We do not use your location to create a user profile, track your movements, or personalize advertising.

You can deny or revoke location permission at any time through your device settings. The rest of the App can still be used without granting location permission.

## Data stored on your device

The App stores certain information locally on your device to remember your preferences and improve performance.

This may include:

- Selected language (Albanian/English)
- Selected country
- App preferences
- Cached fuel price data
- Cached nearby-station results

When you use the nearby stations feature, cached station information may also include the approximate search coordinates associated with that request.

This information is stored locally on your device and can be removed by clearing the App's data or uninstalling the App.

## Data the App fetches

The App fetches fuel price information from publicly available online data sources. The App may display the source of this information inside the interface.

The nearby stations feature uses external mapping and geographic data services to retrieve fuel stations around the location you request.

## Advertising

The App displays ads and uses third-party advertising SDKs, such as Google Mobile Ads / AdMob.

These SDKs may automatically collect or process certain device and usage information in order to serve and measure ads. This may include:

- IP address used for network communication
- Approximate location inferred from IP address, depending on the advertising provider's settings
- Advertising and interaction data, such as impressions and clicks
- Basic device information, such as device model and operating system version
- Device or advertising identifiers where permitted by your device settings and applicable law

We do not use your precise location from the nearby stations feature to personalize advertising.

You can reset or limit ad personalization from your device settings:

- Android: **Settings → Privacy → Ads** (wording may vary by Android version)

## Analytics

The App does not currently use a separate in-app analytics product to track user behavior beyond measurement that may be performed by advertising SDKs.

## Sharing

We do not sell your personal data.

Information may be processed by service providers when necessary to operate App features, including:

- Hosting and station lookup services
- Mapping and geographic data services
- Advertising providers

When you use the nearby stations feature, the location coordinates needed for the request may be transmitted to these services in order to return nearby station results.

## Data retention

Karburanti Sot does not maintain user accounts or a database of user location histories.

Nearby-station information may be cached temporarily on your device.

Technical service providers may retain limited request or diagnostic information according to their own security, operational, and retention practices.

## Security

The App does not use user accounts or passwords.

Network requests are made using HTTPS where supported to protect information while it is transmitted.

No internet-based service can guarantee absolute security, but we limit the information used by the App to what is necessary to provide its features.

## Children’s privacy

The App is intended for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the App's functionality, services, or data practices change.

The latest revision date will be displayed at the top of this page.
